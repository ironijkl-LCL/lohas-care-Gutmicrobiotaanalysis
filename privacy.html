隱私政策

樂活健康系統 · 腸道微生態檢測
最後更新：2026 年 10 月

1. 概述

本 App（「樂活腸道」）由 Lohascare Gerontech 提供。我們非常重視你的私隱，尤其是健康相關資料。

2. 我們收集什麼資料？

我們不收集任何個人資料或健康資料。

所有評估答案、日誌、歷史記錄、設定均只儲存在你裝置的本地儲存（localStorage）內，不會上傳到任何伺服器。

3. 資料用途

本地資料僅用於：顯示評估結果與個人化建議、產生趨勢圖與信號燈、家庭分享連結（只含分數摘要）、記住語言與大字模式等本機體驗。

4. 資料分享

我們不會將任何資料出售、出租或分享給第三方。家庭分享功能由你主動產生連結並發送，資料仍只存在你的裝置。

5. 權限說明

  • 麥克風：僅在你主動使用語音功能時使用，音訊不會被儲存或上傳。
  • 相機 / 相片：僅在你主動使用「拍照識餐」時使用，圖片只在本機分析，不會上傳。

6. 資料刪除

你可隨時在 App 內清除歷史與日誌。卸載 App 或清除資料即可完全刪除所有本地資料。

7. 兒童私隱

本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，亦不會故意收集兒童資料。

8. 香港法例

我們遵守香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》。由於本 App 不收集個人資料，因此不涉及資料使用者登記等要求。

9. 醫療免責

本 App 提供的評估、建議與運動處方僅供健康生活方式自我參考，並非醫療診斷、治療或預防疾病的建議。如有健康疑慮，請諮詢醫生。

10. 聯絡我們

如有私隱相關查詢，請透過 App 內「關於」或你取得本 App 的渠道聯絡我們。

Privacy Policy

Lohas Health · Gut Microbiome Check
Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This App (“Lohas Gut”) is provided by Lohascare Gerontech. We take your privacy seriously, especially regarding health-related information.

2. What data do we collect?

We collect no personal or health data.

All assessment answers, daily logs, history and settings are stored only in local storage on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

3. How data is used

Local data is used solely to show results and personalised suggestions, generate trend charts and traffic lights, create family share links (score summary only), and remember language / large-text preferences on your device.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Family sharing is activated only when you generate and send a link; data remains on your device.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only when you actively choose voice features; audio is not stored or uploaded.
  • Camera / Photos: used only for the optional meal-photo feature; images are analysed on-device only and never uploaded.

6. Deleting data

You can clear history and logs at any time inside the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing data permanently removes all local data.

7. Children’s privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Hong Kong law

We comply with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance of Hong Kong. As this App does not collect personal data, registration as a data user is not required.

9. Medical disclaimer

Assessments, suggestions and exercise prescriptions are for lifestyle self-reference only. They do not constitute medical diagnosis, treatment or advice. Please consult a doctor for any health concerns.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through the App or the channel from which you obtained this App.
