隱私政策

Lohascare GutSafe 樂活食得安
最後更新：2026 年 10 月

1. 概述

本 App（「樂活食得安」）由 Lohascare 提供，定位為家庭照顧者飲食安全與腸道健康初篩參考工具，並非醫療診斷或醫療器械。

2. 我們如何處理資料

問卷答案、運動打卡、緊急聯絡電話、語言與大字設定：
只儲存在你裝置的本地儲存（localStorage），不會上傳到我們的伺服器。

餐膳相片（你主動使用「影相驗餐」時）：

當你選擇分析餐膳相片，相片會被傳送至我們的分析服務（目前託管於 Cloudflare Workers），僅用於產生該次質地與吞嚥安全參考建議。我們不會用這些相片建立個人檔案、不會出售，亦不會用於廣告。

分析完成後，我們不會長期保存可識別你身分的相片內容。若服務暫時無法連線，App 會改用離線參考模式，相片不會被傳送。

3. 權限說明

  • 相機：僅在你主動拍攝餐膳時使用。
  • 相簿：僅在你選擇已有相片進行分析時使用。
  • 麥克風：僅在你主動使用語音輸入或語音提問時使用；音訊不會被儲存或上傳。

4. 資料分享與第三方

我們不會將你的資料出售或用於廣告追蹤。餐膳分析服務僅用於完成你當次要求的質地參考分析。家庭分享功能由你主動產生文字報告並透過 WhatsApp 等自行發送，資料控制權在你手上。

5. 資料刪除

你可隨時在 App 內清除本地歷史與日誌。卸載 App 或清除 App 資料即可刪除裝置上的所有本地記錄。如需查詢分析服務端的資料處理，可透過下方聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

6. 兒童私隱

本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，亦不會故意收集兒童資料。

7. 香港法例與醫療免責

我們遵守香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》。本 App 提供的評估與建議僅供健康生活方式自我參考，不能取代專業醫療診斷或言語治療師的指導。有持續不適請諮詢醫生或言語治療師。

8. 聯絡我們

私隱相關查詢，請電郵：privacy@lohascare.example（請改成你真實會收信的地址）

Privacy Policy

Lohascare GutSafe
Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This App (“Lohascare GutSafe”) is provided by Lohascare as a lifestyle screening and caregiver reference tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis.

2. How we handle data

Quiz answers, exercise logs, emergency contact, language and large-text settings:
Stored only in local storage on your device and not uploaded to our servers.

Meal photos (when you actively use photo analysis):

When you choose to analyse a meal photo, the image is sent to our analysis service (currently hosted on Cloudflare Workers) solely to generate texture and swallowing-safety reference suggestions for that request. We do not build personal profiles from these photos, do not sell them, and do not use them for advertising.

We do not retain identifiable photo content long-term. If the service is unavailable, the App falls back to offline reference mode and the photo is not transmitted.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you take a meal photo.
  • Photo Library: used only when you select an existing photo for analysis.
  • Microphone: used only for voice input features you initiate; audio is not stored or uploaded.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your data or use it for ad tracking. The analysis service is used only to fulfil your request. Family sharing is activated only when you generate and send a report yourself.

5. Deleting data

You can clear local history and logs inside the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes all local data. For questions about the analysis service, contact us below.

6. Children’s privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13.

7. Hong Kong law & medical disclaimer

We comply with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Assessments and suggestions are for lifestyle reference only and do not replace professional medical or speech-therapy advice.
